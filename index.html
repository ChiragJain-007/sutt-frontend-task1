Got questions?

Why do I need a link in bio tool?

Most social platforms only let you add one link. A link in bio page lets you share all your content, shops and socials from that single link.

Is Linkstack free?

Yes. The free plan covers unlimited links and basic customization. Paid plans add analytics, scheduling and more themes.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on paid plans you can connect a custom domain so your page lives at your own URL.

How many links can I add?

As many as you like. Reorder them anytime by dragging, and hide or schedule links when you need to.
